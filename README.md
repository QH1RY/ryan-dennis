## Positioning
Ryan Dennis is the founder of Photobooths London and BXP — Brand Experience Partnership — and co-founder of Quirky Hire. The site is designed as his long-term personal authority hub for brand engagement, experiential marketing, trade shows, event activation psychology, entrepreneurship and future speaking/consulting.

## SEO / AEO foundations
- Semantic HTML homepage
- Person, WebPage, WebSite and Organisation JSON-LD
- LinkedIn and Instagram `sameAs` entity connections
- Canonical URL
- Open Graph metadata
- Robots.txt
- XML sitemap
- Mobile responsive design
